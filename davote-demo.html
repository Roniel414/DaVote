Platform Capabilities
WHAT DAVOTE™ DOES
Six revenue-generating features in one platform
🗳
Real-Time Fan Voting
Fans vote live during listening parties, album drops, and tour announcements. Results update instantly — no refresh needed.
📊
Artist Analytics Dashboard
Artists and labels see exactly what fans want — by track, by market, by demographic. Data that drives smarter A&R decisions.
🎵
Setlist & Single Selection
Let fans vote on what gets played at the next show or what drops as the next single. Fan demand becomes a marketing tool.
💰
Monetized Voting Events
Artists host premium voting events. Fans pay to participate. Multiple revenue streams for artists and the platform.
🔐
Label & Brand Integration
Labels embed DAVOTE™ into campaigns. Brands sponsor voting events. Enterprise API for enterprise deals.
📱
Mobile-First Platform
Built for fans on the go. Works on any device, any browser. No app download required. Instant access via link.
The Process
HOW IT WORKS
Four steps from setup to insight
01
Artist Onboards
Artist or label sets up their DAVOTE™ profile and creates their first voting event in minutes.
02
Fans Get the Link
Artist shares the event link via social media, email, or at the listening party. Fans click — no download needed.
03
Fans Vote Live
Fans cast votes in real time. Results update instantly. The crowd's voice shapes the moment.
04
Artist Gets Data
The artist and label see exactly what fans want. Real data. Real decisions. Real growth.
