Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: 2025 | DAVOTE™ | Sylvester Enterprises

You can ask DAVOTE™ to delete the data we hold about you at any time.

HOW TO REQUEST DELETION
1. Email info@davote.app with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
2. Include the name or email address on your artist account, or the Facebook/Instagram account you connected to DAVOTE.
3. We will confirm your request and delete your account data within 30 days. Vote data from fan sessions is anonymous and is automatically removed 90 days after an event.

IF YOU CONNECTED FACEBOOK OR INSTAGRAM
You can also remove DAVOTE's access yourself: in Facebook go to Settings & privacy > Settings > Business integrations (or Apps and Websites), find the DAVOTE app, and choose Remove. When you remove the app, we stop receiving data from your account, and any Meta-derived data we stored is deleted on request as described above.
